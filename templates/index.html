Accessibility + Mobility Support

Safer movement, one step ahead.

EdgeSense is a wearable accessibility-focused mobility support system that helps users detect stairs, curbs, ledges, and sudden drop-offs before a dangerous misstep happens. Designed for both indoor and outdoor use, it supports safer movement in hallways, stairwells, sidewalks, campus spaces, and low-visibility environments.

Why It Matters

Walking hazards are often below us, not just in front of us.

Many mobility and safety systems focus on forward-facing obstacles, but stairs, curbs, ledges, and sudden elevation changes are some of the most dangerous and overlooked walking hazards.

EdgeSense is built to provide an extra layer of awareness before a dangerous step happens, helping users move more confidently through both familiar and unfamiliar spaces.

What the system supports

  • Indoor stairwells and hallways
  • Outdoor sidewalks and curbs
  • Campus and building navigation
  • Low-visibility movement support
