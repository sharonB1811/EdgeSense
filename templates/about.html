About EdgeSense

An accessibility-focused system for safer, more independent movement

EdgeSense helps users detect drop-offs like stairs, curbs, and ledges in real time, providing reliable navigation support in both daylight and low-visibility environments.

The Problem

Many mobility and safety systems focus on obstacles directly in front of a person, but some of the most dangerous hazards are actually below them. Stairs, curbs, ledges, and sudden changes in elevation are common in both indoor and outdoor environments.

These hazards are especially dangerous for individuals with visual impairments, mobility challenges, or those navigating unfamiliar environments. A single missed step can lead to serious injury.

Existing solutions often rely heavily on vision-based systems, which fail in low-light or nighttime conditions—leaving a critical gap in accessibility and safety.

Where hazards occur

  • Indoor stairwells and hallways
  • Building entrances and ramps
  • Outdoor sidewalks and curbs
  • Low-light or unfamiliar environments

The Solution

EdgeSense is a wearable mobility support system that continuously measures ground distance to detect elevation changes before a user takes a step. When a drop-off or hazard is detected, the system provides immediate feedback to help prevent falls.

Unlike traditional systems, EdgeSense does not depend on lighting conditions. By using distance-based sensing, it remains effective in both bright and dark environments, making it a reliable accessibility tool for everyday use.

In addition to hazard detection, EdgeSense includes user-controlled safety features like quick check-ins and emergency alerts to trusted contacts.

What makes EdgeSense different

  • Real-time drop-off detection (stairs, curbs, ledges)
  • Works in both day and night conditions
  • Light-independent sensing for reliability
  • Built-in emergency alert and check-in system

How It Works

Ultrasonic Sensing

Continuously measures distance to the ground to detect sudden changes in elevation.

Real-Time Alerts

Notifies the user immediately when a potential hazard is detected.

User Safety Controls

A built-in button allows users to send quick check-ins or emergency alerts.

Context Awareness

Optional camera and dashboard provide additional environmental context during alerts.

Impact

EdgeSense improves accessibility by giving users greater awareness of their environment, helping prevent falls and increasing confidence in daily movement.

By working independently of lighting conditions, it ensures consistent support across different environments—indoors, outdoors, day, or night.
