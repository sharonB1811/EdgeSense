About

An accessibility-focused system for safer walking in everyday spaces

EdgeSense is designed to help users notice dangerous walking hazards like stairs, curbs, and sudden drop-offs before a misstep happens.

The problem

Many existing mobility and safety tools focus on what is directly in front of a person, but some of the most dangerous hazards are actually below them. Stairs, curbs, ledges, and sudden changes in walking surface height are common in both indoor and outdoor environments.

These hazards can lead to trips, falls, and injuries for a wide range of people, including visually impaired users, elderly individuals, students, and anyone navigating unfamiliar spaces.

Common hazard areas

  • Indoor stairwells and hallways
  • Building entrances and ramps
  • Outdoor curbs and sidewalks
  • Low-visibility or unfamiliar areas

The solution

EdgeSense is a wearable mobility support system that detects sudden changes in ground level and warns the user before they take a dangerous step. It combines sensing, feedback, and simple safety actions to improve walking accessibility in everyday environments.

While the system provides extra value in low-light conditions, its main purpose is broader: helping users move more safely and confidently indoors and outdoors.

What makes it different

  • Focuses on drop-offs and elevation changes
  • Works across indoor and outdoor spaces
  • Combines accessibility support with safety actions
  • Built for fast, understandable feedback
