Project Summary

EdgeSense is a wearable accessibility and mobility support system that helps users detect stairs, curbs, ledges, and sudden drop-offs before a dangerous misstep occurs.

Designed for both indoor and outdoor movement, the project combines hardware sensing, live dashboard feedback, and safety actions like check-ins and emergency alerts.

By relying on light-independent sensing, EdgeSense remains useful in both daytime and nighttime environments, helping make everyday navigation safer and more accessible.
